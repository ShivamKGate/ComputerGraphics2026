Alien Planet 18

Shivam Sinay Kharangate

A purple alien world built only from cubes. Level 0: one 2×2×2 cube. Level 1a: flat cube grid (translation). Level 1b/c: y-scale heights, vertex colors, orbiting camera. Level 2: color + height map sampling (start low-res, raise toward target). Level 3: sky islands, volcano cubes, UFOs on cubic Bézier paths. Level 4: fog, crystal lights, rippling ground, comets (creative stretch).
